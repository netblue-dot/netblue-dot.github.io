ANALYST ASSESSMENTMalacca carries the largest oil flow of any chokepoint and saw little disruption from the Hormuz crisis in H1 2026. The main risks are traffic concentration, collisions and a wider South China Sea or Taiwan contingency. Alternatives through Sunda and Lombok add days to a voyage.
