Red Sea and chokepoint tracker

A replay of daily ship traffic through Suez, Bab el-Mandeb and the Cape of Good Hope from September 2023 to the latest data, with the Strait of Hormuz, trade volume estimates and vessel types alongside. Every chart moves on one shared timeline. Index 100 is each route's January to October 2023 average.

Cross-check against the World Bank

The World Bank Data Lab published changes in vessel calls for the first months of the crisis, using the same PortWatch data. This table computes the closest equivalent from the live data. The baselines differ (World Bank: January 2022 to October 2023 by day of year; here: January to October 2023), so expect similar direction and size, not identical numbers.

Monthly average index

Average daily transits in the month divided by the route's January to October 2023 average, times 100. The latest month may be incomplete.

Event log

Each event links to its source. Incidents without a confirmed attacker and government claims are labelled as such. Events dated after the latest PortWatch data appear in this table only.

Data provenance

Sources: UN Global Platform; IMF PortWatch. Counts are ships detected, not cargo tonnage, and trade volumes are estimates. Rerouting has several causes, so these charts show timing and not cause.
